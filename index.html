About Tathra Tides

Tathra Tides is a stylish two-bedroom retreat in the heart of Tathra, just steps from the beach and the town's favourite cafés. Wander through Balinese-inspired gardens, soak in the private outdoor sauna, or fire up the BBQ as the sun goes down. Coastal trails, mountain biking, and some of the best surf on the Sapphire Coast are all on your doorstep.
